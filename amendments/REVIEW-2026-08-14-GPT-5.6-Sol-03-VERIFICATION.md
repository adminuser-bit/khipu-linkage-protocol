### 1. **MAJOR — A21/A22.2; the N2 finite-support denominator is mathematically wrong.**

**Failure scenario:** A22.2 now defines a legitimate clustered mixture: choose one decoy document uniformly, then choose one admissible permutation uniformly **conditional on that document**. But A21 defines effective N2 support as S_2 = Σ_d M_1(d).
That would be appropriate only if every distinct `(document, permutation)` state received equal probability. It does not. Under A22.2, a state belonging to document (d) has probability 1/(D·M_d).
If (M_d) varies across documents, the support atoms are unequal. Therefore Σ M_d does **not** establish the claimed finite-resolution floor.
A conservative support criterion consistent with your N1 logic is D × min_d M_d > 1000m, because the largest individual atom is 1/(D·min M_d).
At m=250,000 and D=25, that means every decoy document used by a scoreable triple needs at least M_d > 10,000,000 admissible images.
**Required fix:** Replace the N2 portion of A21 with:
> **For N2, let (D) be the number of matched decoy documents and (M_d) the number of distinct admissible A11 permutation images of decoy (d). Because A22.2 samples documents uniformly and then images uniformly conditional on document, N2 support is probability-weighted, not S_2=Σ_d M_d. The conservative finite-support scoreability condition is D·min_d M_d > 1000m, equivalently the maximum null atom 1/(D·min_d M_d) < 0.001/m. The sum of permutation counts may be reported descriptively but is not an effective-support denominator.**

### 2. **MINOR — A20.6 arithmetic itself is correct once Finding 1 is fixed.**

For the requested worked example m=250,000: 0.001/m = 4.0×10⁻⁹. A20.6 sets N = 10,000m = 2,500,000,000. With the add-one estimator, zero exceedances gives p_min = 1/2,500,000,001 = 3.9999999984×10⁻¹⁰, almost exactly **one-tenth of the first Holm threshold**. So Monte Carlo resolution has a 10× margin.
For N1, A21's M_1 > 1000m gives M_1 > 250,000,000, which is correct under the registered conservative support logic. The **clustered-mixture construction itself no longer pseudoreplicates documents**: repeated Stage-2 samples are Monte Carlo draws from a defined equal-document mixture, not attempts to pretend that permutations are independent document identities. The only remaining arithmetic problem is the weighted-support formula in Finding 1.
**Required fix:** Finding 1 only.

### 9. **MAJOR — A24.2; the invocation wrapper commits locally before invocation but does not require a remote push before the model call.**

**Failure scenario:** A24.2 says the wrapper "commits" the prompt hash before invocation and archives outputs afterward. Until that pre-invocation commit is pushed to the protected record, a one-operator workflow can discard/rewrite local history and retain only the favored invocation.
This is not a complaint that a determined operator can violate the rules—any protocol can be violated. It is a mechanical hole in the mechanism intended to make silent reruns detectable.
**Required fix:** Change A24.2(b)–(c) to:
> (b) creates **and pushes** to the protected record a commit containing the monotonic run ID, prompt hash, model ID, and decoding settings before invoking the model, and verifies that the remote contains that commit; if the push fails, the invocation may not occur;
> (c) immediately after return, error, abort, or timeout, automatically hashes the complete raw output/error state and creates and pushes an append-only result commit before another registered invocation may begin.
