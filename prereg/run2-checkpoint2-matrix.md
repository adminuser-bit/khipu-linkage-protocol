## Falsification verdicts (full)

### Linear A — **WEAKENED**

*Claim attacked:* E2: KU-RO arithmetic, held-out splits over ~1,370-1,427 documents, and reproduction of Linear B toponyms constitute independently checkable evidence for decipherment-relevant hypotheses

The flagship 'external anchor' is circular: proposed phonetic values are required to reproduce Linear B toponyms (pa-i-to etc.), but the values themselves are retrojected from Linear B sign homomorphy ('Sign values are partly inferred by retrojection from Linear B', run1 dossier Linear A §2; one scorer concedes 'a prior, not an independent check'). A value table built from Linear B necessarily regenerates Linear B-like toponyms; passing that test carries near-zero bits. The KU-RO check is retrospective: KU-RO='total' was itself derived FROM sum consistency, so re-running arithmetic validates only the already-settled numeral system; the live hypothesis surface it constrains is the narrow klasmatogram/fraction band (Corazza 2021 values per Wikipedia Linear A). Held-out document splits exist but the documents are ~5-sign formulaic administrative records (7,150-7,396 tokens total, range Braović CL 50(2) vs Nepal & Perono Cacciafoco Information 15(2):73), so held-out folds share formulae and vocabulary with training — low discriminative content. For the questions the field cares about (family affinity, L5-L6), E2 is explicitly conditional on H-LNA-0 shadow validation, and the best available evidence predicts failure: Tamburini's own benchmark collapses to 47.5% on the solved Luwian/Hittite pair as information conditions degrade (Frontiers in AI 2025, DOI 10.3389/frai.2025.1581129), and no cognate-matching method has ever validated at ~7.4k tokens with zero confirmed cognate pairs — the restart-exploitation critique applies directly to any method tuned on Linear B ground truth. Data basis is also compromised: the only licensed corpus (SigLA, CC BY-NC-SA) ships as a 2.5MB undocumented escaped-octal JS blob (audit machine_readiness 2); the only convenient bulk corpus (lineara.xyz) has no LICENSE file and EFA-copyrighted images (audit machine_readiness 4, unlicensed), yet H-LNA-1 stability clause requires running BOTH corpus variants. E2 survives only in the structural/numeral band (L1-L4 claims on held-out real accounting documents plus prospective SigLA ingestions, changelog live to June 2026 per audit); it is effectively E1 for phonetic/family claims pending a shadow-validation pass nobody has demonstrated.

### Cretan Hieroglyphic — **REFUTED**

*Claim attacked:* E2 (marginal): numeral/fraction arithmetic on the clay subcorpus and cross-corpus sign-identity predictions against Linear A constitute independent checks

Downgrade to E1. Both claimed E2 instruments fail on inspection. (a) Arithmetic: the decimal numeral system and fraction partition were established by CHIC (Wikipedia, Cretan hieroglyphs); no live hypothesis class remains that clay-document arithmetic would adjudicate, and the entire arithmetic surface is ~274 sign-groups / 723 sign tokens on clay (1989 census, same source) — re-verification of settled numerals is not a decipherment-relevant check. (b) Cross-corpus sign identity vs Linear A is not independently checkable: Linear A is itself unread, so a proposed CH=LA sign identity has no external semantic ground truth — the palaeographic match IS the hypothesis, and with ~96-135 CH signs (CHIC 96 syllabograms vs Evans ~135, dossier §1) matched against 97 LA signs under flexible shape criteria, the look-elsewhere problem swallows any unregistered match; no G3-admissible consequence (object type, quantity, independently attested name) follows from a match. (c) The object of study is unstable: the script/decoration boundary is actively contested and the 2024 CUP volume reclassifies formerly 'decorative' seal signs as script (BMCR 2026.01.17), so corpus membership and the sign inventory — the preconditions of any held-out protocol — are not fixed (G8 violation). (d) Data: CHIC has no digital edition (CEFAEL stops before Études Crétoises 31, audit); Younger's KU site is NXDOMAIN-dead (audit). Correction to the audit: linear0.xyz IS live (verified this session) and serves CH inscriptions — but its transcriptions are credited to '(J)onathan Younger' with images © École Française d'Athènes, i.e., a derivative of the defunct unofficial Younger pages, hosted in github.com/mwenge/linear0.xyz (CretanHieroInscriptions.js) with NO LICENSE file and no CHIC apparatus — this does not deliver a citable data basis. With ~1,555 total sign tokens in 2-3-sign groups, contested corpus membership, no external ground truth for the one live claim type, and no licensed machine-readable edition, the exercisable claims are method-development claims on deciphered benchmarks (Linear A/B ground truth) — the E1 condition. E2 restorable only after CHIC is re-keyed, a boundary decision is frozen, and a registered cross-corpus test with decoy sign-pairings is specified.

### Cypro-Minoan — **WEAKENED**

*Claim attacked:* E2 for the CM1/CM2/CM3 classificatory claim (H-CM-1) via held-out inscription-level splits with registered separation/unity controls

The test design is real but its independence and executability are both compromised. (a) The annotated sign-image dataset is withheld: PLoS Data Availability statement and repo README verbatim — 'The Cypro-Minoan dataset is unfortunately unavailable, as it is covered by copyright' (github.com/ashmikuz/sign2vec_d; audit machine_readiness 1). Only frozen embeddings (vector.h5) and models are public. Running H-CM-1 on those vectors is not an independent test: the embeddings were produced by the same team whose published conclusion ('a unitary, single Cypro-Minoan script', Corazza et al. 2022, PLoS ONE e0269544) the hypothesis adjudicates — cropping, normalization, and training choices are inherited unaudited, so a unity result on their vectors is correlated evidence, not corroboration (G7 analog at the artifact level). (b) Confound structure: the corpus is hand-drawn from published editions (Braović et al. 2024 flag hand-drawn datasets as a systemic caveat), and the registered secondary endpoint (substrate/site model vs group-label model) faces near-collinearity — CM2 is essentially the Enkomi clay-tablet corpus (1,732 of 2,899 instances on clay; CM2=1,430, CM3=316 per Corazza et al.), so 'substrate beats group label' may be statistically undecidable at n=2,899 with CM3 underpowered, a scenario the prereg only partially anticipates (CM3 restriction clause). (c) The E1 preconditions require Linear B and Cypriot-syllabary drawing datasets downsampled to CM sizes; no such licensed drawing dataset was audited to exist — precondition executability is unverified. (d) Independent replication requires rebuilding the dataset from copyrighted drawings (Olivier HoChyMin, Ferrara CMI). Additional negative fact found this session: the lineara.xyz sister explorer linearc.xyz ('Cypro-Minoan') is dead — the domain 302-redirects to a GoDaddy for-sale page — so no unofficial bulk corpus exists either. E2 stands for the classificatory claim only, contingent on either dataset reconstruction or explicit acceptance that the test inherits the claimants' preprocessing; sign-value claims remain bracketed (Linear A/B ancestors, deciphered Cypriot-syllabary descendant per Steele) but are above this corpus's power (<250-300 inscriptions, <3,500-4,000 sign tokens, range preserved).

### Eteocypriot — **REFUTED**

*Claim attacked:* E2: four bilinguals fix external semantic content, and the unpublished balance of Olivier's 1,397-entry IG XV corpus constitutes genuinely held-out real text 'that exists today'

Downgrade to E1. The load-bearing held-out instrument is factually obsolete: all three scorers cite the 'unpublished' IG XV corpus via a Kyprios Character notice describing it as 'in preparation', but Inscriptiones Graecae XV Pars I (Inscriptiones Cypri syllabicae), Fasciculus I — Inscriptiones Amathuntis Curii Marii — was published by De Gruyter in 2020 (BMCR 2021.02.24; De Gruyter serial page). That fascicule is precisely the Eteocypriot partition: per the BMCR review, 'most of the so-called Eteocypriot texts were found in Amathous, among them some of the longest ever discovered'; the Amathous corpus adds 65 inscriptions (nos. 19-83) beyond Masson 1961, including two bilingual dedications to Kypria by King Androkles — and the review notes many new items 'consist of minimal text'. So the Eteocypriot heartland material is published and in-sample for any future claimant; the still-unpublished fascicules (Paphos 464, other sites 547 per the corpus notice) are overwhelmingly Greek-syllabic, not Eteocypriot. With the held-out partition gone, what remains is: (i) four bilinguals that have been in every claimant's training data for a century (Amathus bilingual known since 1913/1914, stone now lost per Wikipedia — even autopsy re-verification is impossible); (ii) a securely classifiable core of ~26 inscriptions (Steele per BMCR 2015.01.24) vs Wikipedia's 'several hundred' — an unresolved order-of-magnitude range on which no affiliation test has statistical power against a multi-family candidate panel (Tyrrhenian, Hurrian, NW Semitic, substrate — all live per Wikipedia); (iii) zero machine-readable data (audit: Kyprios Character is a coin DB; IG XV is a $400 print volume). Every exercisable evaluation is therefore a method claim on deciphered benchmarks (E1). New excavated bilinguals would restore E2/E3 but are unschedulable. The scorers' E2 rested on a held-out corpus that stopped existing in 2020.

### Proto-Elamite — **UPHELD**

*Claim attacked:* E2 for accounting-structural/semantic claims via entries-must-sum-to-totals arithmetic and held-out splits over ~1,600-1,755 digitized tablets

I attempted four attacks; the E2 rating (already correctly restricted to accounting-structural/semantic claims, with language/phonology capped at E1 per the glottography caveat, Kelley Cambridge Element) survives all of them. (1) Data-access attack: fails — the audit verified CDLI live export end-to-end (1,755 catalogue entries, 1,597 with sign-level M-code ATF, 508KB download verified 2026-08-11, machine_readiness 5); this is the only system in the set where the held-out protocol is executable today on licensed-open structured data. (2) Circularity attack: the six numerical systems were validated by exactly this arithmetic decades ago (Englund 2004), so re-running it proves nothing — but the check still genuinely constrains NEW claims (header/entry/total parsing, commodity-class and institution-sign semantics must respect system-commodity associations) on withheld tablets, and the predictions are G3-admissible (numerical quantities, object type). ### Linear Elamite — **WEAKENED**

*Claim attacked:* E2: Desset et al. 2022 readings are checkable against independently attested cuneiform Elamite, with anchored-text-derived values tested on remaining texts as held-out material

E2 stands only as an adjudication program with three material defects the scorers under-weighted. (1) Anchor contamination: the gunagi/kunanki silver vessels that anchor the Shilhaha/Ebarat/Sewe-palar-huhpak readings come from the unprovenanced Mahboubian private London collection ('commercial excavations' in the 1920s-30s per family claim); scholars initially dismissed them as likely forgeries, and Desset himself was only convinced of authenticity by the decipherment working — an epistemically circular authentication (LRB, Stevenson, 'Beyond Mesopotamia'); one identified fake is already in the corpus (Desset et al. 2022 count 'about 40 LE texts (including one identified fake)', ZA 112). If any anchor vessel is inauthentic, the 13-step value-derivation chain is poisoned, and the program has no instrument to resolve authenticity. Jacob Dahl has published a 'stinging critique' alleging methodological errors (LRB) — reception is genuinely split, as Wikipedia records ('others remain sceptical until detailed translations... have been published'). (2) No held-out partition: the 72 values were derived iteratively over texts covering 96.3% of all 1,890 sign occurrences (Desset et al. 2022) — the derivation consumed the corpus; the 'remaining' material is the ~3.7% unread residue. This is the restart-exploitation configuration (Tamburini/G5): iterative refitting against the full corpus with unlogged search paths. The only genuinely external evidence is the cuneiform-Elamite lexicon/morphology, and a global decoy test (attested-Elamite yield of the 72 values vs ≥999 scrambled value assignments) is runnable — that keeps E2 alive — but whether 40-63 texts (range: Desset 40 / Wikipedia 51 / Elamicon 63 records, audit) can discriminate Desset's full-phonographic model from Mäder's ~30%-logogram model at any registered threshold is unknown; no power analysis exists. (3) Data: Desset released NO machine-readable dataset (audit machine_readiness 0 for the ZA 2022 publication); the sole digital corpus is Mäder's Elamicon — 63 fragment records in custom PUA encoding, license explicitly unsettled, last commit 2024-06-12 (github.com/elamicon/elamicon). Verification, not decipherment, is the licensed program, and even that must first register how it handles unprovenanced anchors.

A reserved 20% folio block is therefore not an exchangeable sample from one generating process — depending on which folios are reserved, the holdout can differ from the training block by scribe and 'dialect', so a classifier verdict on the reserve tests scribe/section composition as much as generating class. The prereg registers transliteration robustness but NOT scribe/Currier-language stratification of the reserved block; without it, the 'held-out internal evidence' is internally confounded. (2) The single-class framing of H-VOY-1 ('one class posterior ≥0.90') is ill-posed against this heterogeneity: a manuscript with five hands and two statistical languages may be a mixture, and the prereg has no mixture outcome. (3) Battery feasibility: class (c) requires ≥30 length-matched (~37k-token) human-gibberish texts per the 2022 Yale-style elicitation; published human-gibberish samples are far shorter, so length-matching may force algorithmic generation, reviving the G4 red-team problem the scorers themselves flag. None of this restores E3 or licenses readings; it means the E2 is real but the registered design has an unregistered confounder, and the scorers' own favored outcome (an indeterminate/sub-0.85 null) is even more likely than they state.

### Khipu — **WEAKENED**

*Claim attacked:* E2 with demonstrated external documentary ground truth via the Medrano & Urton Santa Valley khipu-revisita match, and a cheap E3 route via hash-deposited predictions (H-KHI-2).

The OKR data leg is the strongest in the whole set and I could not dent it (audit opened khipu.db v2.1.0 and counted directly: 619 khipus, 54,403 cords, 110,677 knots, MIT license, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.18025748). Four attacks on the evidence structure partially land. (1) The positive calibration pair is itself unadjudicated: Medrano & Urton 2018 is titled 'TOWARD the Decipherment...' (Ethnohistory 65(1)) — a proposed correspondence (132 tributaries vs 133 six-cord groups) discovered retrospectively with matching rules chosen after seeing both sides; no independent replication or formal statistical vetting of the pair has appeared, and my search found no published critical test either way. Using an unconfirmed match as the mandatory positive control (H-KHI-1: 'pipeline must recover the published match') is circular — a pipeline could 'pass' by reproducing an artifact. (2) The look-elsewhere problem is worse than scored: the colonial archive side is neither digitized nor enumerated, so the Holm denominator (G5 search log) is defined only over documents the team happens to inspect — the true multiplicity across Peruvian archival holdings is unknowable, and Tamburini-style restart exploitation (re-running linkage with adjusted grouping rules until a match appears) is exactly how a retrospective match of this shape can be manufactured; the prereg's freeze-before-search rule mitigates this prospectively but cannot rehabilitate the 2018 pair. (3) Population mismatch: OKR's 619 khipus are ~37% of the ~1,650-1,700 documented (Khipu Field Guide census), and only colonial-era khipus with surviving paired documents are linkable at all — the Santa Valley set may be a near-unique preservation accident, as one scorer concedes; no second match has surfaced since 2018. (4) E3 remains a designed-but-never-executed instrument. E2 stands — the permuted-archive null design is genuinely falsifiable and the data are open — but 'demonstrated external ground truth' overstates a single unreplicated retrospective correspondence.

### Zapotec — **REFUTED**

*Claim attacked:* E2 on the ground that calendrical day-name/year-bearer models generate checkable predictions on monuments not used to form them, with archaeological dating as an external check.

Should fall to E1 (method/benchmark claims only), with the calendrical-chronology work explicitly credited as archaeology rather than decipherment evaluability. Grounds: (1) The checkable core is the already-solved part. The pan-Mesoamerican 260-day calendar and year-bearer positions (2-7-12-17) are the anchored, effectively deciphered stratum (Urcid, FAMSI 'Zapotec Writing' essay); predictions about day-sign placement on new monuments test THAT solved stratum, not any undeciphered-residue hypothesis. Beyond calendrics and name captions nothing is read, there is no biscript, and no completed proto-Zapotecan reconstruction (Urcid, same source) — so there exists no candidate decipherment hypothesis for which held-out real evidence could currently be scored, which is the definition of the E2 gate. (2) The celebrated external check — the 496-221 BCE lunar-day-count rechronology (Latin American Antiquity) — tests epigraphic CHRONOLOGY against archaeological dating; it neither confirms nor refutes any sign-value or reading hypothesis. Counting it toward decipherment evaluability is a category error all three scorers repeat. (3) No frozen test is constructible: there is no published total corpus count (all three scorers concede this; my search found none — only Urcid's 2001 monograph, its reviews, and page scans on archive.org), no transliteration standard, no sign database, and no neutral adjudicator: any 'prediction confirmed on a new monument' would be scored by the same single-scholar reconstruction (Urcid's) that generated it, with unfrozen matching rules — precisely the horoscope logic and restart-exploitation pattern the plan's §2 and G5 bar. A test whose sampling frame, metric, and judge are all undefined is not 'held-out real evidence' in the prereg's sense. E1 activities (digitizing Urcid 2001, building a sign inventory, benchmarking on the solved calendrical stratum) are exactly what the scorers' own key_uncertainties describe: 'a 12-month effort is corpus-building, not modeling.' That is an E1 program, and the rating should say so.

### Isthmian/Epi-Olmec — **WEAKENED**

*Claim attacked:* E2 on the ground that the falsification protocol was 'actually executed': Houston & Coe applied Justeson-Kaufman values to the Teotihuacan-style mask and got incoherence.

I found direct counterevidence to the executed-protocol narrative as the scorers tell it. Justeson and Kaufman published their own extended READING of the very same held-out text: 'Epi-Olmec ritual practices referred to in hieroglyphic texts, with special reference to the text on a Teotihuacan-style mask,' in Tiempo Detenido, un Tiempo Suficiente (Wayeb Publication Series vol. 1, 2018, pp. 59-139; confirmed in Justeson's University at Albany CV, presented earlier at the 2004 Texas Symposium). So the same mask yields 'incoherence' to the critics (Houston & Coe, as reported in Macri's 2010 review) and an ~80-page coherent ritual text to the authors. This means the historical 'execution' adjudicated nothing: with no frozen coherence metric, no decoy values, and unlimited interpretive degrees of freedom, apply-values-to-new-text degenerates into dueling expert judgment — the exact restart-exploitation failure mode (Tamburini) the prereg is built against, and a live instance of the non-specificity problem (arXiv 2608.02999: multiple incompatible 'readings' can each look internally coherent). Additional unscored defect: the mask is an unprovenanced collection piece, so its evidentiary standing as a falsification instrument is compromised for BOTH sides. What keeps E2 alive rather than refuted: the Long Count dates are securely read (real anchors); genuinely held-out shorter texts exist (Tuxtla statuette — whose find-spot was even archaeologically re-investigated, Latin American Antiquity 'Environs of Tres Zapotes as the Find-Spot of the Tuxtla Statuette'); and a FROZEN version of the protocol (decoy-ranked coherence under prereg G5) is constructible from the Kaufman-Justeson 2001 transliterations, albeit with the conflict that the data release and the hypothesis are the same party. But the scorers' strongest sentence — 'the clearest demonstrated falsification protocol of any contested American decipherment' — is not sustainable: the demonstration is contested at its core, and Vonk's information-theoretic underdetermination argument (Wikipedia, 'Isthmian script') stands unanswered at ~12 texts.
