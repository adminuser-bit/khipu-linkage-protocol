# Run 3: Red-Team Findings & Claim-Band Matrix

## Red-team findings (41; fixes for FATAL and MAJOR findings are binding on any execution of the designs)

### FATAL

- **[oracle-bone]** H-OBS-2 decoy arithmetic makes the registered threshold unattainable, guaranteeing a family null that §8 then markets as a strong negative. With ≥99 decoys, the minimum empirical p is (1+0)/(1+99) = 0.01 exactly, which does not satisfy the frozen 'empirical p < 0.01' (prereg §3 H-OBS-2), and Holm correction across all proposals scored drives the required threshold to 0.01/m (0.005 with just two proposals) — unreachable at rank-1-of-100 resolution. PA-8's gloss ('rank-1-of-100 test has size 0.01 exactly by construction') silently converts p<0.01 into p≤0.01, a widening of a frozen threshold, prohibited by the freeze block ('appendices may narrow but never widen'). The sibling designs caught exactly this hole (Meroitic P-4 narrowed to 999 decoys with cap m≤9; Maya uses 4,999 with K≤25); OBS did not. As written, either no proposal can ever pass (the §8 claim that a family null 'upper-bounds what current methods can deliver anywhere in the program' becomes dishonest — the test was unpassable by construction) or scoring proceeds under the widened p≤0.01 reading (protocol violation).
  - **Required fix:** Narrow the decoy count to ≥999 (preferably 4,999) per proposal and register a proposal cap m such that 1/(D+1) ≤ 0.01/m (e.g., 999 decoys with m ≤ 9, or 4,999 with m ≤ 25), mirroring MER PA-4 / MAY §7. Update PA-5 and PA-8 accordingly and re-verify Holm attainability arithmetic in the deposited appendix before any H-OBS-2 generation.
- **[khipu]** H-KHI-1's Holm denominator and its empirical-p resolution are mutually incompatible, and the search extent is unfrozen — so the design either guarantees a null or hands the team a discretionary lever. The Holm denominator is 'the count of all (khipu, document, grouping) triples scored' (Phase A1.4/B), and Phase B.1 says the prioritization heuristic 'cannot exclude' any of U1. If the search approaches the enumerated universe (619 khipus × 4 groupings × ≥100 documents ≈ 2.5×10^5 triples, plus every LLM-triage suggestion counted per §8), the Holm floor is 0.001/m ≈ 4×10⁻⁹, while the frozen draw counts (9,999 N1; ≥10,000 N2) resolve p only down to ~1×10⁻⁴ — no pair can ever pass, and the §7 'multiplicity-honest upper bound on the linkage rate' is vacuous because the registered test was arithmetically unpassable. Conversely, because the number of triples actually scored is not fixed ex ante ('it affects cost, not validity'), the team can stop scoring early and shrink the denominator — optional stopping on the Holm correction, precisely the restart-exploitation hazard G5/Tamburini targets. The PA power analysis derives entry-count floors but never checks Holm attainability.
  - **Required fix:** At Phase A0, freeze the exact scoring extent (full cross-product over the frozen universe, or a registered deterministic subsample) so the Holm denominator m is fixed before any scoring. Then make the threshold attainable under that m: register a two-stage architecture (screening pass at the frozen draw counts, logged; confirmatory pass on screen-survivors with null-draw counts ≥ 10·m/0.001 or an exact/saddlepoint tail computation, method frozen in the appendix), and add the attainability demonstration (min achievable Holm-corrected p < 0.001/m) to the deposited power analysis.
- **[proto-elamite]** H-PE-3b/4b decoy null is degenerate and anti-conservative. Decoys are category labels 'drawn frequency-matched from K's category set' — but K (Englund 2004 + Kelley commodity/personnel/institution identifications) contains on the order of 10–30 distinct categories, so the 999 'decoys' are ~50x-duplicated copies of ~10–30 distinct score values. The true chance of the proposed category strictly beating all alternatives is ~1/|categories| (~0.03–0.1), yet the endpoint reports it as empirical p ≤ 0.001 and feeds it into Holm at family α=0.01. Worse, the proposal generator (which may reason over the same corpus contexts the 3a association model scores) can simply propose the best-fitting category for each sign, making the p-floor pass automatic under H0. With ≤10 proposals scored, the family has roughly a 30–60% chance of producing ≥1 'significant' E2 semantic candidate that carries essentially zero evidence beyond 'argmax category fits best' — exactly the horoscope-logic failure the program's H-ONO-1 template (999 DISTINCT decoy names) was designed to exclude. Ties with duplicated decoys additionally make the p ≤ 0.001 = 1/1000 floor formally unreachable unless tie-handling is specified.
  - **Required fix:** Replace the decoy-category null before any data contact: (i) split each proposed sign's attested contexts into fit/held-out sets by frozen rule; the 3a model's category assignment must be fixed on the fit set and scored only on held-out contexts; (ii) construct the null at the sign level — compare the proposed sign's held-out context-fit under category c against ≥999 frequency-matched OTHER signs' fits under c, and/or a structure-preserving permutation of the proposed sign's context-system associations (A11-analog); (iii) recompute and deposit the true chance level, tie-handling rule, and Holm arithmetic in appendix A.4; (iv) require the generator agent to be sealed from the 3a association model's fitted parameters.

### MAJOR

- **[oracle-bone]** The A6 prospective subset — the design's PRIMARY contamination instrument — is structurally biased against itself and can be mechanically forced to fire (or be empty). Characters whose accepted decipherment postdates T_c are, in the pinned HUST-OBC Figshare v3 (2024 release), filed among the 9,411 UNDECIPHERED categories: (a) their images sit unmasked in the training set (unlike standard holdouts, whose images are fully masked — an asymmetric train/test condition), (b) their modern identities may be absent from a retrieval head built over the deciphered inventory's identity space, making prospective Top-10 structurally depressed or zero, and (c) no procedure is registered for mapping award-batch characters (China Daily 2016 / Henan 2024 sources) to HUST-OBC category IDs. A mechanically forced >5-point deficit reads as leakage under A6 and quarantines a clean result; and if T_c lands at 2024+ (any modern LVLM component), the enumerable post-cutoff set may be near-zero, with no registered minimum-n rule — the primary G6 instrument silently evaporates. PA-8 already concedes ~30% false-flag at n≈25.
  - **Required fix:** Register in PA-6: (1) the award-character → HUST-OBC-category mapping procedure, palaeographer-verified; (2) masking of prospective characters' images from all training artifacts, identical to holdout masking; (3) the retrieval label space as full CJK Unified + Extensions codepoints (not the deciphered-inventory identities); (4) a minimum prospective-subset size (e.g., n ≥ 15), below which the design declares in advance that absolute-accuracy E1 claims are unlicensed rather than tested; (5) the exact-binomial CI decision rule alongside the 5-point point-estimate rule.
- **[oracle-bone]** Internal contradiction on the A19 disagreement rule decides the headline claim. §2 freezes: 'If the treatments disagree on any registered success criterion, no positive claim is licensed.' §3.1/PA-4 license 'method advance' if 'U-A passes after Holm AND U-B agrees in direction.' Direction-agreement is strictly weaker than criterion-agreement: U-B at +0.1 points, non-significant, fails the registered success criterion while agreeing in direction — §3.1 licenses the claim, §2 prohibits it. This is exactly the kind of execution-time discretionary degree of freedom the amendments exist to close, on the single most consequential adjudication in the family.
  - **Required fix:** Harmonize to one rule and state it identically in §2, §3.1, and PA-4: an E1 method-advance claim requires BOTH unit treatments to pass their Holm-corrected registered tests; U-A-pass-plus-U-B-direction licenses at most a treatment-disagreement finding reported per A19.
- **[oracle-bone]** The contamination controls miss the dominant leakage channel — image→identity memorization — and §7.6's 'paired-arm symmetry' defense is false as stated. Re-keying and the 100-ID memorization probe only address ID→character mapping recall; but HUST-OBC/EVOBC images have been public since 2024, so any pretrained LVLM re-ranker can recall a held-out character's modern identity from the glyph image itself. Evolution-chain masking removes images from the pipeline's training artifacts, not from upstream pretraining. Critically, the two arms do NOT share pretrained components (the CipherOBS rerun is a ConvNeXt-based generative-retrieval reimplementation; only the pipeline arm carries an optional LVLM re-ranker), so contamination differentially inflates the pipeline arm — the comparative E1 'method advance' claim, which §7.6 declares protected, is exactly the claim contamination can manufacture. A wrong-but-plausible 'advance over SOTA' passes every registered check except the noisy prospective subset (30% false-flag, n≈25).
  - **Required fix:** Extend PA-6's memorization probe to an image→identity cloze: for every pretrained component in either arm, query 100 randomly sampled held-out-character images (seed pinned) for their identities before scoring; register that any component with above-chance recall is either removed from the arm or the affected arm's absolute AND comparative results are licensed only via the prospective subset. Correct §7.6 to state that arm symmetry holds only for shared components, and record per-arm component manifests.
- **[maya]** The frozen incoherence rule mechanically rejects TRUE readings of well-attested signs, and PA-8's 'family null is evidential' computation ignores it. An occurrence is 'incoherent' if below the 1st percentile of the known-sign score distribution, and any single incoherent occurrence rejects the proposal (§4.5). For a correct proposal behaving like a known sign, each occurrence independently falls below that threshold with p≈0.01, so P(false rejection) ≈ 1−0.99^n: ~26% at 30 occurrences, ~63% at 100. The best-attested panel signs — precisely the scoreable ones under the ≥10-occurrence bar — are the most likely to be falsely killed. PA-8's P(family null | ≥10 correct proposals) ≤ 0.4^10 models only the decoy-rank prong, ignoring both the incoherence prong and the sealed-prediction prong; MAY-P1's gate (rank-1 recovery) never exercises the incoherence rule either. A family null under this rule is far weaker evidence than the registered G9 interpretation ('sealed-holdout methods cannot advance single-sign decipherment') claims.
  - **Required fix:** Recalibrate the incoherence operationalization in PA-7 before data contact: per-sign threshold percentile = α/n (α frozen, e.g., 0.05, Bonferroni over the sign's n occurrences), or a min-score statistic calibrated on known signs stratified by occurrence count. Recompute PA-8 power including all three conjunctive prongs, and run the full prong battery (including incoherence and a mock sealed prediction) inside MAY-P1 so the gate measures end-to-end sensitivity.
- **[maya]** Glyph-blinded mode is defeated by cleartext metadata. Blinding remaps grapheme codes only (§6.2), but each occurrence row G sees carries real text ID or site/provenance, object type, date, and the accepted readings of co-occurring known signs (§4.2.1). An LLM-assisted generator can re-identify famous passages (site + date + surrounding readings uniquely identify most well-published monuments), recover the masked sign's identity and published proposals from memorized literature, and even anticipate sealed iconography — so MAY-P1 can be passed by memorization and 'only blinded-mode results license E2' (§6.2) is unsafe. The X leakage audit scans only for sealed occurrence IDs, sealed text IDs, and verbatim sealed content — inferential deanonymization through visible metadata is invisible to it. The blinded/unblinded divergence measurement cannot detect this failure because both modes would be contaminated equally.
  - **Required fix:** In the blinded corpus mount: replace site/provenance and text IDs with opaque tokens, coarsen dates to bands, and stratify rather than name object types. Add a registered re-identification probe run by X before generation: a probe model is given blinded visible rows and asked to name the true sign/text; above-chance recovery (frozen chance band) voids blinded-mode E2 licensing for this run and is reported as a contamination finding.
- **[maya]** Scorer training contaminates the calibration gate. PA-7 freezes the scorer as trained on 'visible, non-panel MHD sequences (grapheme-code tokens + accepted-value side channel).' The 20 MAY-P1 calibration signs are known-value and NOT panel members, so their occurrence contexts — with their true values attached via the side channel — are in the scorer's training data. When MAY-P1 then masks these signs and scores candidate values, the scorer has literally seen the answer in training: gate recovery is inflated, the ≥12/20 pass is near-meaningless as a transfer estimate for residue signs (whose contexts the scorer genuinely never saw), and PA-8's use of the gate rate as per-proposal detection power propagates the inflation into the family-null interpretation.
  - **Required fix:** Amend PA-7 before deposit: the scorer is trained excluding all occurrences of both panel signs AND the 20 calibration signs (and their value side-channel entries), making calibration-sign contexts genuinely held out. State explicitly that the same scorer weights are used for calibration and residue scoring.
- **[khipu]** The degenerate-N1 escape hatch widens a frozen threshold. Frozen H-KHI-1 requires empirical p < 0.001 on BOTH nulls (within-document permutation AND same-genre decoys). Phase B.3 rules that when the admissible permutation group yields <100 distinct images, 'N1 is declared underpowered for that pair... and N2 becomes the binding null.' But in exactly that situation N1's minimum empirical p exceeds 0.001 (with <100 distinct images, min p > 1/100), so the frozen text makes such pairs unpassable — and the design's rule converts unpassable into passable-on-N2-alone. That is a widening of a registered threshold authored inside the design, prohibited by the freeze block and by the design's own role statement ('this document... authors no adjudication criteria').
  - **Required fix:** Replace the rule: a pair whose N1 admissible group cannot resolve p < 0.001 (fewer than 1,000 distinct images) is reported as unscoreable under the frozen criteria and cannot support a positive linkage claim this run; alternatively, file a dated pre-data-contact amendment to the tribunal record explicitly modifying H-KHI-1's dual-null requirement for degenerate cases.
- **[khipu]** The N2 null is diluted and mismatched. Frozen null (b) is '≥100 same-genre decoy documents'; the design's operationalization builds the ≥10,000-draw N2 distribution as 100 decoy identities plus 9,900 N1-permuted decoy variants — 99% of the null mass is permuted material, not the registered decoy-document class. Worse, §2.2 makes published mid-16th-century visita editions (Huánuco 1562, Chucuito 1567) the 'primary backbone of the decoy panel because transcription cost is low,' while realistic match candidates are 17th-century revisitas/padrones from different regions: systematic period/region/entry-count differences between decoys and candidate documents make the decoy null easier to beat than a genre-matched null, inflating false-positive linkage. The eligibility rule (Peru-wide, 1532–1700, ≥20 entries) imposes no per-candidate matching.
  - **Required fix:** Add to the frozen appendix: (1) a passing pair must also rank 1st among the 100 pure decoy-document identity scores (permutation-padded p reported alongside); (2) the decoy panel is stratified, and each candidate document is scored against decoys matched on period band (±50 years), region/audiencia, document type, and entry-count stratum, with the stratification frozen at A0.
- **[khipu]** The Santa Valley positive-control criterion is unfrozen — a discretionary hole on the family's quarantine trigger. The frozen prereg says failure to recover the published match = pipeline defect, all family results quarantined; but neither the design nor the appendix defines 'recover': at what threshold (p < 0.001 on both nulls? uncorrected or Holm-corrected over the full search?), under which grouping (G-2? all four?), against which null panel? Under the Holm-corrected reading the control may be unpassable purely by multiplicity arithmetic (see the denominator finding), mechanically quarantining the whole family; under an uncorrected reading the choice is made at execution time. Either way, the single event that can void every family result is adjudicated by an unregistered rule.
  - **Required fix:** Freeze in the A17 appendix before Phase B: the positive control passes iff the six Corongo khipus vs the 1670 revisita achieve empirical p < 0.001 on both nulls, uncorrected, under the pre-frozen matching rules and grouping G-2 (color-band six-cord), scored outside the Holm family as calibration-only; specify the null panel used and that any other grouping's result is reported descriptively.
- **[meroitic]** H-MER-2's null interpretation is dishonest against the design's own audit citation, and the licensing arm is unspecified. The 20% comparison constant is the published method's accuracy ON TRAINING DICTIONARIES; its unseen-term accuracy was 0% (audit row; §3.1 quotes both). Testing leave-k-out (held-out) accuracy against a train-set constant means a pipeline achieving, say, 15% on genuinely unseen glosses — a qualitative advance over the published 0% floor — is registered as confirming that 'the embed-and-align family fails' (§11), converting a method advance into a headline negative. - **[meroitic]** Arm-S defeats the fold-masking leakage control through the synthetic corpus. - **[meroitic]** The lexeme decoy pool is gameable by the generator through gloss-string choice. Log G's raw string and the normalized form.
- **[proto-elamite]** H-PE-1 gold labels are circular with the model input. Gold {header, entry, subtotal, total} is a deterministic function of frozen positional+composition rules plus the arithmetic sum-check — computed from exactly the feature families (line position, N/M token composition, sign identities) that §6.2 supplies to the models. ### Conversion notes

CONVERSION NOTES — ambiguities exposed by the A18 reissue (format conversion only; each item flags where band assignment required a convention, for Run 3 sign-off, not re-adjudication):

1. Voynich structural band. The tribunal adjudicated only the class question ("E1 — model-comparison only"). Whether structural claims (L1–L3) on held-out folios could hold a separate E2 was never separately adjudicated, and the unregistered scribe/Currier stratification confounder cuts against it. I cap structural at E1 consistent with the adjudication; an E2 structural band would need a fresh adjudication.

2. Khipu language-status band. "E2 now; E3 path (A12)" attaches to the record-linkage instrument (numeric band). No adjudication exists for a "do narrative khipus encode language" band; entered as E1 by convention. The A12 E3 instrument, if executed successfully, would bear on this band, but that inference was never made by the tribunal.

3. "No route" and "barred" are not E-classes. Proto-Elamite's "no phonetic/language-identity route" and Proto-cuneiform's calibration bar (confident language verdict = pipeline failure) do not map onto E0–E3; the A18 format lacks a symbol for route-absent or design-barred bands. I rendered Proto-Elamite phonetic as E1-cap (matching the matrix's "language capped E1") and Proto-cuneiform language-status as "barred by design." Recommend the final report adopt "barred" as an explicit fifth symbol.

4. Verification-program E2s cannot be split by band. Linear Elamite (Desset value table) and Isthmian (Justeson–Kaufman value tables) hold E2 only as adjudication of an existing claimant's system, in which phonetic values, language identification, and semantic coherence stand or fall jointly. The bands are marked jointly; reading them as independent per-band licenses would overstate the adjudicated status.

5. SW/Tartessian: the band boundary runs through "phonetic." Abecedary-constrained sign-value hypotheses sit inside the licensed L1–L3 signary E2; language-level phonology/readings are E1-at-best. A single phonetic/lexical cell forced a split entry.

6. "Solved" marker. For shape-C/E systems (Meroitic, Etruscan, Iberian branches, Eteocypriot, Eteocretan, OBS, Maya) the phonetic band is closed by accepted prior scholarship. "Solved" is used as a status, not an E-class; conflating it with E3 would be wrong (no program instrument produced it).

7. Bare-class systems. For systems adjudicated at a single letter with no band scoping (Byblos, Rongorongo, Vinča, Zapotec, Eteocretan, Eteocypriot, Cretan Hieroglyphic), all bands inherit the class as a cap. Within an E1 cap, the semantic-readings band licenses method claims on deciphered benchmarks only — never readings of the real corpus. This is a stated convention of the conversion, not a new adjudication (the alternative, marking those semantic bands E0, would itself have been an unlicensed adjudication).

8. Khitan large: "E2 in principle, E1-blocked in practice" has no A18 symbol. Rendered as a dual entry. Any Run 3 capacity decision should read the exercisable class (E1), not the epistemic one.

9. Meroitic language-status (NES classification): never separately adjudicated; single-scholar (Rilly) comparanda dependence and the live Rowan alternative are registered caveats. Entered E1 by convention.

10. Maya remains E2-conditional: the corpus-audit precondition is "closer to dischargeable" (Glyph Dwellers R75) but not certified discharged (tabulator note 6); the residue figure must travel as the sourced range 10–15% vs ~1/3 of 1,210.

11. NE Iberian: the E2 cell is valid only with the Luo 75% quarantine attached; any pre-scoring appendix citing that figure before full-PDF verification violates A17.

12. Long tail (Table 2) was never blind-scored or falsifier-attacked; classifications are indicative conversions from Run 1 inventory + Run 2 patch data. Four long-tail systems carry recent unadjudicated decipherment claims (Dhofari, Kushan, Sidetic expansion, Rohonc cipher claim) — if any is promoted into scope, it enters at E0–E1 until this program's own instruments are registered.
